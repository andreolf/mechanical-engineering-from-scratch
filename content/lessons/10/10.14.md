Most of the world's electricity, from coal, gas combined cycles, nuclear, geothermal and solar thermal plants, comes from steam turbines. The loop is the **Rankine cycle**: pump water to high pressure, boil and superheat it, expand the steam through a turbine, condense it back to liquid. Because it pumps a liquid instead of compressing a gas, the pump costs almost nothing.

## The four steps

1. **Pump** (1→2): liquid from the condenser to boiler pressure. Work $w_p = v\,\Delta p$, small because liquid volume is tiny.
2. **Boiler** (2→3): heat at constant pressure, through the liquid region, across the dome and into superheat.
3. **Turbine** (3→4): expansion to condenser pressure, ideally isentropic.
4. **Condenser** (4→1): heat rejected at constant pressure, usually to a river, the sea or a cooling tower.

Efficiency is $\eta = (w_t - w_p)/q_\text{in}$. Steam isn't an ideal gas, so states come from tables: find $s_3$ at the turbine inlet, then the exit quality $x_4 = (s_3 - s_f)/(s_g - s_f)$ at the condenser.

## Superheat and reheat

Raising the steam temperature raises the average temperature of heat addition, which pushes efficiency up towards Carnot. It also keeps the turbine exhaust drier. Below about 88 % quality, water droplets erode the last-stage blades. **Reheat** expands the steam part-way, sends it back to the boiler, and expands it again: efficiency rises a little and the exhaust gets much drier.

:::example A 10 MPa plant
Boiler 10 MPa, condenser 10 kPa (45.81 °C), steam at 500 °C. Steam values come from small IAPWS-95 tables in the code; $h_3 = 3375.13$ kJ/kg, $s_3 = 6.5995$ kJ/(kg K).

1. Pump: $0.0010103\cdot(10000 - 10) = 10.09$ kJ/kg.
2. Ideal turbine: $x_4 = (6.5995 - 0.6492)/(8.1488 - 0.6492) = 0.7934$, $h_4 = 2089.7$ kJ/kg, $w_t = 1285.4$ kJ/kg.
3. Heat in 3173.2 kJ/kg, out 1897.9 kJ/kg: efficiency 0.4019 (Carnot between the same extremes: 0.5875). The energy balance closes.
4. With an 85 % turbine: 0.3411, exit quality 0.8740.
5. With reheat at 2 MPa to 500 °C: 0.4167, exit quality 0.9046. With 85 % turbines: 0.3602 and 0.9744.
:::

```python file=rankine.py
"""Rankine steam cycle with superheat and reheat, using small embedded steam tables (IAPWS-95 values) and interpolation."""
import bisect

# superheated steam: (T C, h kJ/kg, s kJ/(kg K))
SUP = {
    10.0: [(350, 2924.02, 5.9459), (400, 3097.45, 6.2141), (450, 3242.34, 6.4219), (500, 3375.13, 6.5995),
           (550, 3501.96, 6.7585), (600, 3625.76, 6.9045), (650, 3748.12, 7.0408)],
    2.0: [(250, 2903.24, 6.5475), (300, 3024.18, 6.7684), (350, 3137.65, 6.9583), (400, 3248.34, 7.1292),
          (450, 3358.22, 7.2866), (500, 3468.24, 7.4337), (550, 3578.97, 7.5725), (600, 3690.74, 7.7043), (650, 3803.75, 7.8302)],
}
# saturated water and steam at the 10 kPa condenser
COND = dict(T=45.81, hf=191.81, hg=2583.86, sf=0.6492, sg=8.1488, vf=0.0010103)
P_COND = 0.01   # MPa


def interp(rows, key, val, out):
    xs = [r[key] for r in rows]
    i = min(max(bisect.bisect_left(xs, val), 1), len(xs) - 1)
    a, b = rows[i - 1], rows[i]
    t = (val - a[key]) / (b[key] - a[key])
    return a[out] + t * (b[out] - a[out])


def h_at(p, T):
    return interp(SUP[p], 0, T, 1)


def s_at(p, T):
    return interp(SUP[p], 0, T, 2)


def expand_to_condenser(h_in, s_in, eta):
    x = (s_in - COND["sf"]) / (COND["sg"] - COND["sf"])
    hs = COND["hf"] + x * (COND["hg"] - COND["hf"])
    h = h_in - eta * (h_in - hs)
    return h, (h - COND["hf"]) / (COND["hg"] - COND["hf"])


def rankine(T_boiler=500, p_boiler=10.0, eta_t=1.0, reheat=None):
    wp = COND["vf"] * (p_boiler - P_COND) * 1000          # kJ/kg, v dp
    h2 = COND["hf"] + wp
    h3, s3 = h_at(p_boiler, T_boiler), s_at(p_boiler, T_boiler)
    q_in = h3 - h2
    if reheat:                                           # expand to 2 MPa, reheat to T
        p_r, T_r = reheat
        h4s = interp(SUP[p_r], 2, s3, 1)
        h4 = h3 - eta_t * (h3 - h4s)
        w_hp = h3 - h4
        h5, s5 = h_at(p_r, T_r), s_at(p_r, T_r)
        q_in += h5 - h4
        h6, x = expand_to_condenser(h5, s5, eta_t)
        wt = w_hp + h5 - h6
    else:
        h6, x = expand_to_condenser(h3, s3, eta_t)
        wt = h3 - h6
    q_out = h6 - COND["hf"]
    return dict(wp=wp, wt=wt, q_in=q_in, q_out=q_out, eta=(wt - wp) / q_in, x=x)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    for label, kw in (("ideal, 10 MPa 500 C", {}), ("turbine 85 %", dict(eta_t=0.85)),
                      ("reheat at 2 MPa to 500 C", dict(reheat=(2.0, 500))), ("reheat, turbines 85 %", dict(reheat=(2.0, 500), eta_t=0.85))):
        c = rankine(**kw)
        print(f"{label}: pump {c['wp']:.2f}, turbine {c['wt']:.1f}, heat in {c['q_in']:.1f}, out {c['q_out']:.1f} kJ/kg; "
              f"efficiency {c['eta']:.4f}, exit quality {c['x']:.4f}")
        assert abs(c['q_in'] - c['q_out'] - (c['wt'] - c['wp'])) < 1e-9          # energy balance closes
    carnot = 1 - (COND["T"] + 273.15) / (500 + 273.15)
    print(f"Carnot between 45.81 and 500 C: {carnot:.4f}")
    assert rankine()['eta'] < carnot
```

:::exercise Sizing a power plant
A plant on the ideal reheat cycle above must deliver 500 MW net. Find the steam flow, the heat rejected in the condenser and the cooling water flow if the river water may warm by only 10 K.
---solution---
1. Net work: $1555.9 - 10.09 = 1545.8$ kJ/kg. Steam flow $500\,000/1545.8 = 323.5$ kg/s.
2. Heat rejected: $323.5\cdot2164.0 = 700.0$ MW, more than the electricity produced.
3. Cooling water: $700\,000/(4.186\cdot10) = 16\,721$ kg/s, about 17 m³ of river water every second. That's why big plants sit on rivers or coasts, or use cooling towers.
:::
