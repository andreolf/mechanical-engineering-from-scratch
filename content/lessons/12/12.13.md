Up to now we treated air as incompressible, and for a cyclist or a kite that's fine. An airliner over the Alps at 870 km/h is a different matter: the air ahead of it is squeezed noticeably, heats up, and its density changes. The measure of how much compressibility matters is the **Mach number**, the flow speed divided by the speed of sound.

## The speed of sound

A weak pressure wave travels through a gas at

$$
a = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial\rho}\right)_s} = \sqrt{kRT}
$$

for an ideal gas, with $k = c_p/c_v = 1.4$ for air and $R = 287$ J/(kg K). Sound speed depends only on temperature: 340 m/s at 15 °C, 295 m/s at cruise altitude. The Mach number is $M = V/a$. Below $M \approx 0.3$ density changes stay under about 5% and the flow can be treated as incompressible.

## Stagnation properties

Bring a moving gas to rest without losses, as at the nose of a pitot tube. The steady-flow energy equation with $h = c_pT$ gives

$$
c_pT_0 = c_pT + \frac{V^2}{2} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + \frac{k-1}{2}M^2 .
$$

If the process is also isentropic, $p\propto T^{k/(k-1)}$ and $\rho\propto T^{1/(k-1)}$:

$$
\frac{p_0}{p} = \left(1 + \frac{k-1}{2}M^2\right)^{k/(k-1)}, \qquad \frac{\rho_0}{\rho} = \left(1 + \frac{k-1}{2}M^2\right)^{1/(k-1)} .
$$

Expanding $p_0/p$ for small $M$ gives $p_0 - p = \tfrac12\rho V^2\,(1 + M^2/4 + \ldots)$: incompressible Bernoulli plus a correction that grows with $M^2$. Stagnation temperature and pressure are what a pitot-static system and a thermometer on the wing actually feel.

:::example An airliner at cruise
At 11 km: T = 216.65 K, p = 22.632 kPa, flying at Mach 0.82.

1. $a = \sqrt{1.4\cdot287.05\cdot216.65} = 295.068$ m/s, so V = 241.956 m/s (871.0 km/h).
2. $T_0/T = 1.13448$: the wing's leading edge sits at $T_0 = 245.785$ K, 29 K warmer than the air. The energy equation $c_pT + V^2/2 = c_pT_0$ checks to $10^{-6}$.
3. $p_0/p = 1.55521$, so $p_0 = 35.198$ kPa.
4. Bernoulli's error, $(p_0 - p)/p$ against $kM^2/2$: −0.25% at M = 0.1, −2.22% at 0.3, −6.02% at 0.5, −14.56% at 0.8.
5. Sound speed is 350.19 m/s on a 32 °C day in Bali and 325.20 m/s at −10 °C in a Swiss winter.
:::

```python file=isentropic.py
"""Compressible flow basics: speed of sound, Mach number and the isentropic stagnation relations for air."""
import math

K, R = 1.4, 287.05
CP = K * R / (K - 1)


def sound_speed(T, k=K, Rg=R):
    return math.sqrt(k * Rg * T)


def stagnation_ratios(M, k=K):
    """T0/T, p0/p, rho0/rho for isentropic flow."""
    t = 1 + (k - 1) / 2 * M * M
    return t, t ** (k / (k - 1)), t ** (1 / (k - 1))


if __name__ == "__main__":
    # an airliner cruising at 11 km (T = 216.65 K, p = 22.632 kPa) at Mach 0.82
    T, p, M = 216.65, 22632.0, 0.82
    a = sound_speed(T); V = M * a
    tr, pr, rr = stagnation_ratios(M)
    T0 = T * tr
    print(f"a = {a:.3f} m/s, V = {V:.3f} m/s ({V*3.6:.1f} km/h)")
    print(f"T0/T = {tr:.5f}: T0 = {T0:.3f} K; p0/p = {pr:.5f}: p0 = {p*pr/1000:.3f} kPa")
    assert abs(CP * T + V * V / 2 - CP * T0) < 1e-6                   # T0/T = 1 + (k-1)/2 M^2 is just the energy equation
    # how wrong is incompressible Bernoulli? compare p0 - p with rho V^2 / 2
    for M in (0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8):
        tr, pr, _ = stagnation_ratios(M)
        exact = pr - 1; bern = K / 2 * M * M                           # both divided by p
        print(f"M = {M}: (p0 - p)/p = {exact:.5f}, Bernoulli {bern:.5f}, error {100*(bern/exact - 1):+.2f} %")
        if M <= 0.3: assert abs(bern / exact - 1) < 0.025                # the usual incompressible limit
    # sound speed rises as sqrt(T): a hot day in Bali versus a cold Swiss winter
    print(f"sound speed at 32 C {sound_speed(305.15):.2f} m/s, at -10 C {sound_speed(263.15):.2f} m/s")
```

:::exercise A pitot tube on a race car
A car drives at 300 km/h through air at 25 °C and 101.3 kPa. Find the Mach number, the exact stagnation-minus-static pressure its pitot tube reads, and the incompressible estimate. How big is the error?
---solution---
1. $a = \sqrt{1.4\cdot287.05\cdot298.15} = 346.15$ m/s; $V = 83.33$ m/s, so $M = 0.2407$.
2. Exact: $p_0/p = (1 + 0.2\cdot0.2407^2)^{3.5} = 1.04116$, so $p_0 - p = 0.04116\cdot101.3 = 4.170$ kPa.
3. Incompressible: $\rho = 101300/(287.05\cdot298.15) = 1.1836$ kg/m³ and $\tfrac12\rho V^2 = 4.110$ kPa.
4. The incompressible formula reads 1.4% low, about $M^2/4$. Good enough for a race car, not for an airliner's airspeed indicator.
:::
